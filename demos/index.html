We're always digging for new drum and bass and jungle, from first-time producers and established names alike.

Before you send

  1. Private, downloadable links

    Send one link to your tracks with downloads switched on. No public links, no attachments.

    SoundCloud privateGoogle DriveWeTransferDropbox

  2. Unsigned and original

    Only send music you own outright that hasn't been released or signed elsewhere.

  3. Your best two to four tracks

    Lead with your strongest track. Finished mixdowns are ideal, but strong ideas are welcome too.

  4. One submission at a time

    We listen to everything that comes in. If we want to take it further, we'll email you back.
